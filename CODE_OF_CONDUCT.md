# Code of conduct

finstats is a small project run in people's spare time. This page says how we treat each other here, what
is not acceptable, and what happens when something goes wrong. It applies to everything under this
repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commit messages, and to anyone acting on the
project's behalf elsewhere.

## What we expect

- **Be kind, and assume good faith.** The person on the other side is giving their time too. Most rudeness
  in issue trackers is tiredness; most "stupid questions" are gaps in the documentation.
- **Criticise the work, not the person.** "This query is slow because…" helps. "Did you even test this?"
  does not.
- **Accept a no.** finstats has a narrow idea of itself (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). A feature
  being declined is not a judgement of you, and a maintainer does not owe anyone an implementation.
- **Give people room.** Not everyone writes English easily, knows Rust, or has run a server before.
  Explain rather than correct; nobody should feel foolish for asking.
- **Own your mistakes**, say so plainly, and move on. Maintainers included.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and demeaning comments of any kind, including those
  about someone's age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality,
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, and pile-ons.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission: addresses, real names
  behind usernames, private messages.
- **Publishing other people's viewing data.** This one is specific to finstats. A database, a backup, a
  Jellystat export, an unblurred screenshot or a log can show what named people watched, when, and from
  which IP address. The people on your server did not agree to have that posted here. Remove or replace
  names, titles, device names and addresses before you paste anything, and never attach those files. Posts
  that contain such data will be edited or removed, whatever the intent.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, write to **business@olayzen.com**. Say what
happened, where (a link helps), and whether you want to be contacted about it. Reports are read by the
maintainer only and are kept confidential; your name is never passed on to the person you report without
your consent.

Please do not take it up in the thread itself: that tends to make things worse for everyone, including
you. (A security vulnerability is a different matter: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).)

## What happens then

The maintainer looks at what happened and chooses the mildest response that stops it:

1. **A private note** asking for it to stop, with an explanation of why, for a first or minor problem.
2. **A public correction**: a comment hidden, edited or removed, a thread locked.
3. **A temporary block** from the repository, for repeated or serious behaviour.
4. **A permanent block**, for sustained harassment, threats, or deliberately publishing someone's private
   or viewing data.

Decisions are made in good faith and explained to the people involved. If you think one was wrong, reply to
the same address and it will be looked at again.
